# Forms

Forms a shop and its buyers fill in, composed from Diba's basics: signing in with a phone, an
address, a discount code and a review. Each is a `Form`, which checks its fields in the page's
language before it is sent.

## Signing in with a phone

A `TextField` for the number, typed left to right, and a button that sends the code; then an
`InputOTP` for the code, the button off until it is whole, and a `Link` to send it again.

<Canvas of={Stories.SignIn} />

## An address

Fields in pairs where they belong together, the pairs one under the other on a phone: who receives
it and their number, the province from a `Select` and the city, the address in a `TextArea` with
what it holds described, and a `Checkbox` to keep it for next time. Save is primary, cancel
quieter.

<Canvas of={Stories.Address} />

## A discount code

The code and its button side by side, their foot on one line; what the shop says of it in an
`Alert` under them.

<Canvas of={Stories.DiscountCode} />

## A review

A score chosen from five in a `ToggleButtonGroup`, the words in a `TextArea`, and the way to send
it, off until there is a score.

<Canvas of={Stories.Review} />
