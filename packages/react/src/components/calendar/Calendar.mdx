# Calendar

A month to pick a day from, drawn in the page. It counts in the reader's calendar, the Persian one
in Persian, and its title opens the years and then the months, to go far in a few presses.

<Canvas of={Stories.Playground} />
<Controls of={Stories.Playground} />

## When to use it

- **A day the reader picks by looking at the month:** a delivery day, a day off, a campaign's start.
- **Where the calendar can stay open** on the page, beside what it decides.

For a date in a form, use a DatePicker: a field that opens a calendar. For a span of days, use a
RangeCalendar.

## Import

```tsx
import { Calendar } from '@kaladan/diba-react'
import { parseDate } from '@internationalized/date'
```

## Anatomy

```tsx
<Calendar aria-label="Delivery day" defaultValue={parseDate('2026-10-17')}>
  <Calendar.Header>
    <Calendar.NavButton slot="previous" />
    <Calendar.YearPickerTrigger>
      <Calendar.YearPickerTriggerHeading />
    </Calendar.YearPickerTrigger>
    <Calendar.NavButton slot="next" />
  </Calendar.Header>
  <Calendar.Grid>
    <Calendar.GridHeader>
      {(day) => <Calendar.HeaderCell>{day}</Calendar.HeaderCell>}
    </Calendar.GridHeader>
    <Calendar.GridBody>{(date) => <Calendar.Cell date={date} />}</Calendar.GridBody>
  </Calendar.Grid>
  <Calendar.YearPickerGrid>
    <Calendar.YearPickerGridBody>
      {({ year }) => <Calendar.YearPickerCell year={year} />}
    </Calendar.YearPickerGridBody>
  </Calendar.YearPickerGrid>
  <Calendar.MonthPickerGrid />
  <Calendar.TodayButton />
</Calendar>
```

## Values

A calendar's values are `@internationalized/date`'s: `parseDate('2026-10-17')`, or
`today(getLocalTimeZone())`. The calendar that draws a value is the locale's, Persian on a Persian
page, but the value itself is one day: its `toString()` is always Gregorian ISO 8601, `2026-10-17`,
whatever calendar showed it as ۲۵ مهر ۱۴۰۵. Keep and send that.

## Examples

### Persian and Gregorian

The locale chooses the calendar. To draw one whatever the language, give the locale a calendar
in React Aria's `I18nProvider`: `fa-IR-u-ca-gregory` for Gregorian in Persian, `en-US-u-ca-persian`
for Persian in English.

### First, last and unavailable days

`minValue` and `maxValue` end the calendar: days outside them cannot be reached. `isDateUnavailable`
says which days within them cannot be picked, a booked day or a holiday, drawn at half.

<Canvas of={Stories.Limits} />

### Years and months

With `Calendar.YearPickerTrigger`, the title opens the years, and a year its months: years away in
three presses. With `Calendar.Heading`, the title is words alone.

<Canvas of={Stories.YearAndMonth} />

### The first day of the week

A week starts on the language's day, Saturday in Persian and Sunday in American English;
`firstDayOfWeek` gives another.

<Canvas of={Stories.FirstDay} />

### Disabled, read-only and invalid

Read-only, the days can be looked at but not picked. Invalid, put an `ErrorMessage` inside the
calendar: it is read with the day picked.

<Canvas of={Stories.States} />

### A week

`visibleDuration` draws a week, `{ weeks: 1 }`, or a few days, `{ days: 3 }`, instead of a month.

## Accessibility

The calendar is a grid of days, each read with its full date. Name it with `aria-label` for what
the day is for. Unavailable days are read as such, and the arrows have their names.

## Styling

`.calendar`; `.calendar__header`, `.calendar__nav-button`, `.calendar__heading`; `.calendar__grid`,
`.calendar__header-cell` and `.calendar__cell` with `[data-selected]`, `[data-today]`,
`[data-unavailable]`, `[data-outside-month]` and `[data-disabled]`.
