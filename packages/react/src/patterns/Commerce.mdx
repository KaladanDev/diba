# Commerce

A storefront's pieces, composed from Diba's basics: a price, a score, a quantity, a product's
card, a line of the cart, an order's summary and a product's page. None is a component of its
own; each is a few basics, and each is written out here to copy.

## A price

The amount in figures of one width, `Text` at `xl` and semibold, with its unit after it in the
muted colour; on sale, the amount in the danger's ink and the old one, struck through in a
`<del>`, over it. Three sizes.

<Canvas of={Stories.Prices} />

```tsx
<Stack gap={0} align="start">
  <del>
    <Text size="xs" tone="muted">
      1,600,000
    </Text>
  </del>
  <Stack direction="horizontal" gap={1} align="baseline">
    <Text size="xl" weight="semibold" tone="danger" style={{ fontVariantNumeric: 'tabular-nums' }}>
      1,240,000
    </Text>
    <Text size="sm" tone="muted">
      toman
    </Text>
  </Stack>
</Stack>
```

## A score

Five stars, as many filled in the gilt as the score rounds to and the rest outlined, with the score
and its count beside them; one `role="img"` names them all, "4.6 out of 5, 124 reviews". A
review's own score is a `ToggleButtonGroup` of five, one chosen.

<Canvas of={Stories.Scores} />

## A quantity

A `NumberField` with its − and +, from one up to what is in stock: at the stock, + is off. Name it
with an `aria-label`, or show its `Label`.

<Canvas of={Stories.Quantities} />

## A product's card

A `Card` with no padding: an `Image` flush along its top, its corners square (`radius="none"`)
under the card's own, with a `Chip` on it for what is special
(a discount, sold out); then, 16 in, its `Card.Title` and `Card.Description`, the price and the
score, and a `Button` across it, off when it is sold out. A picture that does not come says what it
was of. Side by side, each card is as tall as its row, so the buttons line up.

<Canvas of={Stories.ProductCards} />

## A line of the cart

A small `Image`, the name and price, a quantity, and an icon `Button` that removes it, its name in
a `Tooltip`; lines a `Separator` apart, in a `Card`.

<Canvas of={Stories.CartLines} />

## An order's summary

Each line a name and an amount at either end, the total semibold under a `Separator`, and the way
to pay across the card's foot.

<Canvas of={Stories.OrderSummary} />

## A product's page

The picture beside the rest: a `Link` back to its shelf, the name as the page's heading, the
score, the price at `lg`, a `Chip` for free delivery and what is left, the quantity with the way to
the cart, and a quieter link to the buying guide.

<Canvas of={Stories.ProductPage} />
