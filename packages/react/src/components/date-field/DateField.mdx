# DateField

A date, or a date and a time, typed part by part. At rest it reads as a date is written, «۲۵ مهر
۱۴۰۵، ۱۴:۳۰», in the reader's calendar; in focus, its day, month, year, hour and minute can each be
typed or stepped.

<Canvas of={Stories.Playground} />
<Controls of={Stories.Playground} />

## When to use it

- **A date the reader knows and types:** a birthday, an invoice's date, a document's.
- **A date and a time together,** to the hour or the minute.

When the reader picks a day by looking at the month, use a DatePicker: this field with a calendar
behind an icon. For a time alone, use a TimeField; for a span, a DateRangePicker.

## Import

```tsx
import { DateField, Description, FieldError, Label } from '@kaladan/diba-react'
import { parseDate } from '@internationalized/date'
```

## Anatomy

```tsx
<DateField defaultValue={parseDate('2026-10-17')}>
  <Label>Delivery date</Label>
  <DateField.Group fullWidth>
    <DateField.Prefix>…</DateField.Prefix>
    <DateField.Input>{(segment) => <DateField.Segment segment={segment} />}</DateField.Input>
    <DateField.Suffix>…</DateField.Suffix>
  </DateField.Group>
  <Description>The day the parcel leaves the warehouse.</Description>
  <FieldError />
</DateField>
```

## Values

A field's values are `@internationalized/date`'s: `parseDate('2026-10-17')` for a day,
`parseDateTime('2026-10-17T14:30')` for a day and a time, `parseZonedDateTime(…)` for a moment in a
zone. Whatever calendar draws it, its `toString()` is Gregorian ISO 8601: keep and send that. In a
form, its `name` sends the same string.

## Examples

### At rest and typing

At rest, the field shows its date with the month's name, as Flutter's does. Pressed or tabbed
into, it shows its segments: type the digits, or step each with the arrow keys. On a Persian page
they read as a written date does, the day at the right, «روز/ماه/سال», and a time as a clock after
it.

### Granularity

`granularity` is the smallest part it asks: `day`, `hour` or `minute`. `hourCycle` gives a 12-hour
clock with AM and PM, or 24 hours.

### Persian and Gregorian

The locale chooses the calendar: `fa-IR-u-ca-gregory` in React Aria's `I18nProvider` draws
Gregorian on a Persian page.

A zoned value is shown in its own zone. `hideTimeZone` leaves the zone's name out, for a page

### First and last day

`minValue` and `maxValue` make a date outside them invalid. With `validationBehavior="aria"` it
says so at once; by default it waits for the form to be sent. Left empty, `FieldError` says it in
the page's words and calendar, the limit named as the field shows a date: «باید ۲۵ آبان ۱۴۰۵ یا
پیش از آن باشد». Give it your own words, or a `validate` of your own, which goes first.

## Accessibility

Each segment is read with its name, «روز», «ماه», «سال», and its value; the field is named by its
`Label`, described by its `Description`, and its error is read while it is invalid.

## Styling

`.date-field`; `.date-input-group` with `--secondary` and `--full-width`;
`.date-input-group__input`, `__segment`, `__prefix` and `__suffix`; at rest,
`.date-input-group__resting`. `[data-invalid]`, `[data-disabled]` and `[data-focus-within]` on the
group.

## As a block

A tree's DateField block is this field with a calendar behind an icon: what the DatePicker page
shows. Its dates are Gregorian ISO 8601 on the wire, whatever calendar draws them.
