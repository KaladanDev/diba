# ErrorMessage

What is wrong, under something a `FieldError` cannot serve: a group of tags, a calendar, a
one-time code. It is 12 in the danger colour, and it shows whenever it is drawn: you decide when.

<Canvas of={Stories.Playground} />
<Controls of={Stories.Playground} />

## When to use it

- **Under a TagGroup,** which checks nothing itself: "choose one category at least".
- **Under a Calendar or a RangeCalendar** whose day cannot be taken.
- **Under an InputOTP,** joined to it by `aria-describedby`.

In a TextField, a NumberField, a SearchField, a date field or any field that checks its value, use
a `FieldError`: it shows only while the field is invalid, and says what its check found.

## Import

```tsx
import { ErrorMessage } from '@kaladan/diba-react'
```

## Anatomy

```tsx
<TagGroup selectionMode="multiple">
  <Label>Categories</Label>
  <TagGroup.List>…</TagGroup.List>
  <Description>The product is shown in these categories.</Description>
  {none ? <ErrorMessage>Choose one category at least.</ErrorMessage> : null}
</TagGroup>
```

Inside a TagGroup or a calendar it takes their error's place, and they are described by it: a
screen reader reads it with the list or with the chosen day. Elsewhere, give it an `id` and point
the field's `aria-describedby` at it.

## Accessibility

It is text, not an alert: it is read with what it describes, when the reader comes to it. Say what
is wrong and what to do: "Choose one category at least", not "Invalid".

## Styling

`.error-message`: 12 on 16, in `--danger`.

## As a block

A tree has no ErrorMessage of its own: a field's `errorMessage` says it, here a TagGroup's, on the
web and in Flutter.
