# Modal

A panel over the page for a task of its own: a short form, a detail, a choice. The page under it
waits until it is closed, by its buttons, its close button, a press outside or Escape.

<Canvas of={Stories.Playground} />
<Controls of={Stories.Playground} />

## When to use it

- **A short form that belongs to the page,** such as editing an address or inviting a staff
  member, where leaving the page would lose its place.
- **A detail worth a closer look,** such as a size guide or a product's quick view.
- **A choice before going on,** with more to say than a yes or a no.

Not for a question before something that cannot be undone: use an AlertDialog. Not for a record's
details beside a list: use a Drawer, or a Sheet on a phone. Not for a few words about a control:
use a Tooltip or a Popover.

## Import

```tsx
import { Button, Modal } from '@kaladan/diba-react'
```

## Anatomy

```tsx
<Modal>
  <Button variant="secondary">Edit address</Button>
  <Modal.Backdrop>
    <Modal.Container>
      <Modal.Dialog>
        {({ close }) => (
          <>
            <Modal.CloseTrigger />
            <Modal.Header>
              <Modal.Icon>
                <MapPin />
              </Modal.Icon>
              <Modal.Heading>Edit address</Modal.Heading>
            </Modal.Header>
            <Modal.Body>{/* the form */}</Modal.Body>
            <Modal.Footer>
              <Button variant="tertiary" onPress={close}>
                Cancel
              </Button>
              <Button onPress={close}>Save</Button>
            </Modal.Footer>
          </>
        )}
      </Modal.Dialog>
    </Modal.Container>
  </Modal.Backdrop>
</Modal>
```

## Examples

Each example is its own frame: press its button to open it. The main ones open as they load.

### Sizes

`md`, 448 wide, by default; `xs` is 320, `sm` 384 and `lg` 512. `cover` fills the window inside its
margin, and `full` is the whole window, square.

### Placements

`auto` opens it at the bottom of a phone, 16 from the edges where a thumb reaches, and in the
middle from 640 wide, 40 from them.

### A long body

With `scroll="inside"`, the default, the panel stays in the window and its body scrolls between the
heading and the answers. With `scroll="outside"` the panel grows as long as its body and the page
under it scrolls.

<Canvas of={Stories.Scrolling} />

### Controlled

Hold whether it is open with `isOpen` and `onOpenChange`, to open it from anywhere and know when it
closes.

<Canvas of={Stories.Controlled} />

### What closes it

A press outside, Escape, its close button and whatever calls `close`. `isDismissable={false}` on
the backdrop keeps a press outside from closing it, and `isKeyboardDismissDisabled` keeps Escape
from doing so: for a form that would lose its words, leave only its buttons.

<Canvas of={Stories.Dismissing} />

### From any element

A Button is its trigger as it is. Anything else, such as a product's picture, goes in
`Modal.Trigger`, which makes it pressable and lets the keyboard reach it: give it an `aria-label`
when it has no words.

### The rest

`Modal.Header`, `.Icon`, `.Heading`, `.Body` and `.Footer` take their element's props and a
`className`. `Modal.CloseTrigger` is a CloseButton, named “Close” in the page's language. `Modal.Trigger`
takes a `div`'s props.

## Accessibility

It is a `dialog`, named by its heading. Focus moves into it as it opens, stays in it while open,
and returns to its trigger when it closes; the page under it is hidden from a screen reader and
does not scroll.

## Styling

`.modal__backdrop` and its `--opaque`, `--blur`, `--transparent`; `.modal__container` with
`--scroll-outside` and `--full`; `.modal__dialog` with `--xs` to `--lg`, `--cover`, `--full` and
`[data-placement]`; `.modal__header`, `__icon`, `__heading`, `__body`, `__footer`,
`__close-trigger` and `__trigger`. Its paper is the theme's `--overlay`, its shadow
`--overlay-shadow`.

## As a block

A tree opens the same modal, on the web and in Flutter, from the Button in its `trigger` slot and
only from it. It always has its close button; a footer button closes it with `{ "do": "close" }`.
A block's modal is `lg` unless the tree says otherwise.
