# TextArea

Several lines of text: a product's description, a note on an order, a reply to a review. It sits
in a TextField, which gives it its label, its help, its checks and its state, as it gives an Input.

<Canvas of={Stories.Playground} />
<Controls of={Stories.Playground} />

## When to use it

- **Text that runs past a line:** a description, a note, a message, an address written out.
- **Text the reader may break into paragraphs:** Enter starts a new line, it does not send the
  form.

For one line, use a TextField's Input; Enter there sends the form.

## Import

```tsx
import { Description, FieldError, Label, TextArea, TextField } from '@kaladan/diba-react'
```

## Anatomy

```tsx
<TextField maxLength={300}>
  <Label>Product description</Label>
  <TextArea rows={4} />
  <Description>For the buyer.</Description>
  <FieldError />
</TextField>
```

## Examples

### Rows

`rows` is how many lines are drawn before it scrolls. The reader can drag its corner to make it
taller.

<Canvas of={Stories.Rows} />

### Letters left

`maxLength` on the TextField stops the text at that many letters. Show what is left in the
`Description`, from the value's length, so the reader is not stopped without warning.

<Canvas of={Stories.Counter} />

### Direction of the text

`dir="auto"` turns the text with its first letter: a Persian description reads from the right, an
English one from the left, on either page.

<Canvas of={Stories.Direction} />

### Validation

`validate` returns what is wrong, or nothing; `isInvalid` with your own `FieldError` words shows
what the server said. As a TextField's, an empty `FieldError` says what the field's own checks
found, a required field left empty or too few or too many letters, in the page's language.

## Accessibility

The field is named by its `Label`, described by its `Description`, and its `FieldError` is read
while it is invalid. A count of the letters left in the `Description` is read with it.

## Styling

`.textarea` with `--primary`, `--secondary` and `--full-width`; `[data-invalid]`,
`[data-disabled]` and `[data-focused]`. The edge is `--field-shadow`.

## As a block

A tree draws the same field, on the web and in Flutter, three lines tall unless it says `rows`.
Its own checks, required and the fewest and most letters, are written in the page's language.
