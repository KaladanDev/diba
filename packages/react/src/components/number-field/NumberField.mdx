# NumberField

A number the reader types or steps: a quantity, a price, a discount. It is written in the page's
digits and formatted as it is typed, kept between its least and most, and snapped to its step when
the reader leaves it.

<Canvas of={Stories.Playground} />
<Controls of={Stories.Playground} />

## When to use it

- **A count stepped by one:** a quantity, a stock level, a number of days.
- **An amount typed in full:** a price in toman, a weight, a percent off.

For a code or a phone number, which are digits but not amounts, use a TextField with
`inputMode="numeric"`; for a choice along a scale, a Slider.

## Import

```tsx
import { Description, FieldError, Label, NumberField } from '@kaladan/diba-react'
```

## Anatomy

```tsx
<NumberField defaultValue={1} minValue={1}>
  <Label>Quantity</Label>
  <NumberField.Group>
    <NumberField.DecrementButton />
    <NumberField.Input />
    <NumberField.IncrementButton />
  </NumberField.Group>
  <FieldError />
</NumberField>

<NumberField defaultValue={1250000} formatOptions={{ maximumFractionDigits: 0 }}>
  <Label>Price</Label>
  <NumberField.Group>
    <NumberField.Input />
    <span data-slot="unit">toman</span>
  </NumberField.Group>
</NumberField>
```

## Examples

### With and without steppers

− and + suit a count changed by one or two. Leave them out for an amount typed in full, a price or
a weight, and the figure sits at the start.

<Canvas of={Stories.Steppers} />

### Formats

`formatOptions` are `Intl.NumberFormat`'s. A percent takes a fraction: 0.15 is shown as 15%.
Toman has no currency code: format it as a whole number, `maximumFractionDigits: 0`, and put its
word after the figure in a `span` with `data-slot="unit"`. The figure is in the page's digits,
with its separators: digits typed are the page's as they are typed, Latin or Persian, and a decimal
mark with them, so 12.5 typed on a Persian page reads «۱۲٫۵».

<Canvas of={Stories.Formats} />

### Least, most and step

`minValue` and `maxValue` keep it in range; `step` is what − and + and the arrow keys add. A
typed figure is kept in range, and snapped to the nearest step, when the reader leaves the field:
600 becomes 500 with a step of 250. A button is dimmed when its next step would leave the range.

<Canvas of={Stories.RangeAndStep} />

### Validation

`validate` takes the number and returns what is wrong, or nothing. `isInvalid` with your own
`FieldError` words shows what the server said. Left empty, a required field's `FieldError` says
so in the page's language, whatever the browser's.

## Accessibility

The input is a spin button's text box, named by its `Label`. − and + are named «کاهش» and
«افزایش», Decrease and Increase, in the page's language, and are left out of the Tab order: the
arrow keys do their work from the keyboard. A unit's word is drawn beside the figure, not read
with it; name the field with its unit, «قیمت (تومان)», where the label alone does not say it.

## Styling

`.number-field` with `--primary`, `--secondary` and `--full-width`; `__group`, `__input`,
`__decrement-button` and `__increment-button`; `[data-invalid]`, `[data-disabled]` and
`[data-focus-within]` on the group.

## As a block

A tree draws the same field, on the web and in Flutter. `formatStyle` is `decimal`, `percent` or
`currency`; `currency: "IRT"` is toman, drawn as a whole number and its word. `hideSteppers`
leaves out − and +. Its own checks, required and the least and most, are written in the page's
language.
