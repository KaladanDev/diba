# AvatarGroup

Several people at once: avatars stacked, each a quarter over the next, or set out in a grid. After
the first few, a count says how many more, in the reader's digits.

<Canvas of={Stories.Playground} />
<Controls of={Stories.Playground} />

## When to use it

- **Who is on something:** the staff handling an order, the people with access to a store.
- **How many have done something,** a few of them shown: the buyers who reviewed a product.
- **A team at a glance,** in a grid.

To name each person, list them with their avatars beside their names instead.

## Import

```tsx
import { Avatar, AvatarGroup } from '@kaladan/diba-react'
```

## Anatomy

```tsx
<AvatarGroup max={3} size="sm" role="group" aria-label="Who handles this order">
  <Avatar role="img" aria-label="Niloufar Rostami">
    <Avatar.Image src="/staff/niloufar.jpg" alt="" />
    <Avatar.Fallback aria-hidden>NR</Avatar.Fallback>
  </Avatar>
  {/* …more avatars */}
  <AvatarGroup.Count>+5 more</AvatarGroup.Count>
</AvatarGroup>
```

## Examples

### Overlap

A stack sets each avatar a quarter of its width over the next. `clip` cuts each one round the
next, leaving a clear seam on any ground; `ring` rings each in the page's colour instead.

<Canvas of={Stories.Overlap} />

### Max and the count

With `max`, the group draws that many and then the count, `+5` in the reader's digits. Give an
`AvatarGroup.Count` of your own for words, "+5 more", or for a total the page knows but has not
drawn.

<Canvas of={Stories.MaxAndCount} />

### Sizes

The group's `size` sizes its avatars and its count alike.

<Canvas of={Stories.Sizes} />

### The group's look

`size`, `color` and `variant` fill in what each avatar left out; an avatar's own wins. Without a
group colour, each named avatar keeps its own hue.

<Canvas of={Stories.GroupLook} />

### Grid

`isGrid` sets them out 12 apart, wrapping as the room runs out. `overlap` does nothing here.

<Canvas of={Stories.TeamGrid} />

## Accessibility

A group says nothing of itself. Give it `role="group"` and a name for what the people have in
common, "Who handles this order", and name each avatar. Where the words beside it already say it,
"24 people left a review", hide the group (`aria-hidden`) instead. A presence dot and a ring are
for one person at a time: in a stack they would be covered, so leave them out.

## Styling

`.avatar-group` with `.avatar-group--clip`, `--ring` and `--grid`; `.avatar-group__count`. The
overlap is `--avatar-group-overlap`, a quarter of `--avatar-size`.
