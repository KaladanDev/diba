# Tooltip

A few words about what is under the pointer or the keyboard's focus: what an icon button does, what
a term means. It shows after the pointer rests a moment, at once on focus, and goes when either
leaves.

<Canvas of={Stories.Playground} />
<Controls of={Stories.Playground} />

## When to use it

- **An icon button's name,** for the eye as its `aria-label` is for a screen reader.
- **A term or a short rule,** such as when delivery is free.
- **The whole of something cut short,** such as a long product name in a narrow column.

Not for anything a reader must know to go on: a phone has no pointer to rest, so say it on the page
too. Not for anything to press: use a Popover.

## Import

```tsx
import { Button, Tooltip } from '@kaladan/diba-react'
```

## Anatomy

```tsx
<Tooltip>
  <Button variant="secondary">Free delivery</Button>
  <Tooltip.Content placement="top">
    <Tooltip.Arrow />
    Delivery is free on orders over 500,000 toman.
  </Tooltip.Content>
</Tooltip>
```

### How long the pointer rests

`delay` is how long the pointer rests before it shows, 500 milliseconds by default; once one
tooltip has shown, the next shows at once. The keyboard's focus shows it at once. `closeDelay` is
how long it stays once the pointer leaves, 500 by default.

<Canvas of={Stories.Delay} />

### What it describes

A Button, a LinkButton or a Link is described as it is. Anything else, a word or a picture, goes in
`Tooltip.Trigger`, which the keyboard can reach; give a picture an `aria-label`.

<Canvas of={Stories.Triggers} />

### On focus only, or off

`trigger="focus"` shows it only on the keyboard's focus, not under the pointer; `isDisabled` never
shows it.

### The rest

`Tooltip.Arrow` draws a point 12 wide; give it an element of your own as its child to draw another.
`Tooltip.Trigger` takes a `div`'s props.

## Accessibility

Its words describe what it is on: a screen reader reads them after its name. It never takes the
focus and holds nothing to press. An icon button still needs its own `aria-label`: the tooltip
describes it, it does not name it.

## Styling

`.tooltip` with `[data-placement]`, `.tooltip__trigger`; the arrow is
`[data-slot="overlay-arrow"]`, filled with the theme's `--overlay`. Its shadow is
`--overlay-shadow`; how long it waits is `--tooltip-delay` and `--tooltip-close-delay`.

## As a block

A tree puts the same words on one block, on the web and in Flutter. A block that cannot take the
focus, such as Text or an Image, is made one the keyboard reaches. It waits 500 milliseconds, as
one written by hand does, unless the tree says otherwise, and shows above or below only.
