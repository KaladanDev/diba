# Sheet

A panel from an edge of the window that the hand moves: swiped away toward its edge, or dragged
from one stop to the next. Made for a phone, where a thumb does the work: a size to choose, a list
to sort, an order to look over.

<Controls of={Stories.Playground} />

## When to use it

- **A choice on a phone,** such as a size before adding to the cart, or how to sort a list.
- **A long list that need not cover the page at once,** opening at a third of its height and
  dragged up for all of it.
- **A record's details on a phone,** where a Drawer would do on a wider window.

Not for a panel that only opens and closes: a Drawer is simpler. Not for a short task in the middle
of the page: use a Modal.

## Import

```tsx
import { Button, Sheet } from '@kaladan/diba-react'
```

## Anatomy

```tsx
<Sheet>
  <Sheet.Trigger>
    <Button variant="secondary">Order 1041</Button>
  </Sheet.Trigger>
  <Sheet.Backdrop>
    <Sheet.Content>
      <Sheet.Dialog>
        <Sheet.Handle />
        <Sheet.CloseTrigger />
        <Sheet.Header>
          <Sheet.Heading>Order 1041</Sheet.Heading>
        </Sheet.Header>
        <Sheet.Body>{/* the order */}</Sheet.Body>
        <Sheet.Footer>
          <Sheet.Close>
            <Button>Print label</Button>
          </Sheet.Close>
        </Sheet.Footer>
      </Sheet.Dialog>
    </Sheet.Content>
  </Sheet.Backdrop>
</Sheet>
```

## Examples

Each example is its own frame: press its button to open it. The main ones open as they load.

### Edges

From the `bottom`, the default, or the `top`: the window's width, up to 32 short of its height.
From the `left` or the `right`: the window's height, 384 wide or 96% of a narrower one. These are
the window's own edges, whatever the page's direction.

<Canvas of={Stories.Placements} />

### Stops on the way

`snapPoints` are where it stops on its way up, each a share of its height such as `'33%'`, or a
length. It opens on the first, and all of it is always the last; the page under it dims only as it
rises to all of it.

<Canvas of={Stories.SnapPoints} />

### Floating

`isDetached` holds it 8 from every side of the window, round all round.

### What closes it

A swipe toward its edge, a press outside, Escape, its close button and any button in `Sheet.Close`.
`isDismissable={false}` leaves only its buttons.

### Controlled

Hold whether it is open with `isOpen` and `onOpenChange`; a sheet with no `Sheet.Trigger` is opened
only so.

### The rest

`Sheet.Content`, `.Dialog`, `.Header`, `.Heading`, `.Body`, `.Footer` and `.Handle` take their
element's props and a `className`. `Sheet.CloseTrigger` is a CloseButton, named “Close” in the
page's language. `Sheet.Trigger` and `Sheet.Close` each take one Button. `Sheet.NestedRoot` opens a
sheet from inside another.

## Accessibility

It is a `dialog`, named by its heading. Focus moves into it as it opens, stays in it while open,
and returns to its trigger when it closes; the page under it is hidden from a screen reader. The
handle is only for the hand: the close button and Escape do what a swipe does.

## Styling

`.sheet__overlay`; `.sheet__backdrop` and its `--opaque`, `--blur`, `--transparent`;
`.sheet__content` with `--bottom`, `--top`, `--left`, `--right`, `[data-detached]` and
`[data-snap-points]`; `.sheet__dialog` with the same four edges; `.sheet__header`, `__heading`,
`__body`, `__footer`, `__handle`, `__handle-bar` and `__close-trigger`. Its paper is the theme's
`--overlay`.

## As a block

A tree opens the same sheet, on the web and in Flutter, from the Button in its `trigger` slot and
only from it. Its edges are `bottom`, `top`, `start` and `end`, the page's, so `start` is the right
edge of a Persian page; a bottom or top one has its handle, and every one its close button.
