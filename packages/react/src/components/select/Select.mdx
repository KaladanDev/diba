# Select

One option, or several, from a menu that opens under a field: a product's category, an order's
status, a filter. Closed, it shows what is chosen; open, the options, the chosen one highlighted.

<Canvas of={Stories.Playground} />
<Controls of={Stories.Playground} />

## When to use it

- **One of a known list too long to show at once,** four to about twenty: a category, a status,
  a carrier.
- **A filter above a list,** where the label is the list's own.

For two to six options the reader should compare, use a RadioGroup; for a list to search or type
into, a ComboBox; for actions rather than a value, a Dropdown.

## Import

```tsx
import { Description, FieldError, Label, ListBox, Select } from '@kaladan/diba-react'
```

## Anatomy

```tsx
<Select placeholder="Choose a category">
  <Label>Category</Label>
  <Select.Trigger>
    <Select.Value />
    <Select.Indicator />
  </Select.Trigger>
  <Select.Popover>
    <ListBox>
      <ListBox.Item id="scarf">Scarves</ListBox.Item>
      <ListBox.Item id="bag">Bags</ListBox.Item>
    </ListBox>
  </Select.Popover>
  <Description>The product is shown in it.</Description>
  <FieldError />
</Select>
```

## Examples

### Open

The chosen option is on the neutral fill, with no tick, as Flutter's menu marks it; an option
under the pointer is half that fill, so the two never look alike.

<Canvas of={Stories.Open} />

### Placeholder, one and several

`placeholder` shows while nothing is chosen. `selectionMode="multiple"` lets several be chosen,
the menu staying open between them; the field shows them joined with commas, cut with an ellipsis
when they run past it.

<Canvas of={Stories.Values} />

### Sections, icons and descriptions

`ListBox.Section` with a `Header` titles a group of options. An option may lead with an icon and
hold a `Label` and a `Description` for a second line; give it `textValue`, the words it is found
and announced by, when it holds more than words. Closed, the field shows the chosen option's icon,
16, before its words on one line, and leaves its second line in the menu.

<Canvas of={Stories.Sections} />

### Clearable

A `Select.ClearButton` in the trigger empties it while something is chosen; Backspace or Delete
on the field does the same from the keyboard.

<Canvas of={Stories.Clearable} />

### Disabled, invalid, on a grey surface

`disabledKeys` keeps options from being chosen; they are drawn at half.

<Canvas of={Stories.States} />

### In a form

Its `name` sends the chosen option's `id`. A required select left empty stops the form and says
so under it, «این فیلد الزامی است.», in the page's language whatever the browser's.

<Canvas of={Stories.InAForm} />

## Accessibility

The field is a button named by its `Label`, saying what is chosen; the menu is a list box, each
option read with its place and whether it is chosen. A `Select.ClearButton` is for the pointer:
the keyboard empties the field with Backspace.

## Styling

`.select` with `--primary`, `--secondary` and `--full-width`; `__trigger`, `__value`, `__indicator`,
`__clear-button` and `__popover`; `[data-open]`, `[data-invalid]` and `[data-disabled]`. The
options are the ListBox's.

## As a block

A tree draws the same select, on the web and in Flutter, its options `ListBoxItem`s and titled
`ListBoxSection`s; an option's `icon` or `image` shows in the closed field beside its words.
