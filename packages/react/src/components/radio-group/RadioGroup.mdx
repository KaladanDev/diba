# RadioGroup

One choice among a few, every one in sight: the delivery method, a product's type, who pays the
fee. Its Radios are Material's on both platforms, drawn and moving as Flutter's do.

<Canvas of={Stories.Playground} />
<Controls of={Stories.Playground} />

## When to use it

- **One of two to six options** the reader should compare side by side, each with its own help.

For more options, or ones the reader searches, use a Select or a ComboBox; for several at once, a
CheckboxGroup; for two that switch a view, a ToggleButtonGroup.

## Import

```tsx
import { Description, FieldError, Label, Radio, RadioGroup } from '@kaladan/diba-react'
```

## Anatomy

```tsx
<RadioGroup defaultValue="post">
  <Label>Delivery method</Label>
  <Radio value="post">
    <Radio.Content>
      <Radio.Control>
        <Radio.Indicator />
      </Radio.Control>
      <Label>Express post</Label>
    </Radio.Content>
    <Description>45,000 toman, 2 to 4 days</Description>
  </Radio>
  …
  <FieldError />
</RadioGroup>
```

## Examples

### Down and across

`orientation="horizontal"` lays the radios in a row, 16 apart; the label stays above them and the
help under them. The arrow keys follow it.

<Canvas of={Stories.Orientation} />

### Help

Each option's help says what it costs or what follows from it, so the reader compares them where
they choose.

<Canvas of={Stories.Help} />

### None chosen, disabled, read-only

A group may start with none chosen; once one is, the reader cannot clear it, only choose another.
Start with one when there is a sensible default. A disabled option, or a disabled group, is drawn
at half.

<Canvas of={Stories.States} />

### Validation

`isInvalid` with your own `FieldError` words shows what the server said; the rings turn the danger
colour, the words keep theirs.

<Canvas of={Stories.Validation} />

### On a grey surface

`variant="secondary"` fills each ring with the surface, so an empty one shows on a grey card.

<Canvas of={Stories.OnASurface} />

### In a form

Its `name` sends the chosen value. A required group with none chosen stops the form and says so
under it, «این فیلد الزامی است.», in the page's language whatever the browser's.

<Canvas of={Stories.InAForm} />

## Accessibility

The group is a radio group named by its `Label`; each radio is named by its words and read as one
of how many. The keyboard reaches the group once, then moves within it.

## Styling

`.radio-group` with `--primary` and `--secondary`; `.radio` with `__content`, `__control` and
`__indicator`; `[data-selected]`, `[data-invalid]` and `[data-disabled]`.

## As a block

A tree draws the same group, on the web and in Flutter, `orientation` giving a row and each Radio
its `description`. Its own check, one chosen when required, is written in the page's language.
