# CheckboxGroup

Checkboxes under one name, with one value: the list of what is on. The delivery methods a shop
offers, the sizes in stock, the payment methods it takes.

<Canvas of={Stories.Playground} />
<Controls of={Stories.Playground} />

## When to use it

- **Several options, any number of them on:** delivery methods, sizes, payment methods.

For one choice among the options, use a RadioGroup; for a long list, or one the reader searches,
a ListBox or a Select with several choices; for settings that each take effect at once, a
SwitchGroup.

## Import

```tsx
import { Checkbox, CheckboxGroup, Description, FieldError, Label } from '@kaladan/diba-react'
```

## Anatomy

```tsx
<CheckboxGroup defaultValue={['post']}>
  <Label>Delivery methods</Label>
  <Checkbox value="post">
    <Checkbox.Content>
      <Checkbox.Control>
        <Checkbox.Indicator />
      </Checkbox.Control>
      <Label>Express post</Label>
    </Checkbox.Content>
  </Checkbox>
  …<Description>The buyer picks one of these.</Description>
  <FieldError />
</CheckboxGroup>
```

## Examples

### Down and across

Options go one under another. For a row, put them in a wrapping flex row of their own, 16 apart:
on a narrow page it wraps, its rows 32 apart. The label stays above them, the help under them.

```tsx
<div style={{ display: 'flex', flexWrap: 'wrap', gap: 16 }}>{options}</div>
```

### Help

Help under the group, and help under an option of its own, 28 in under its words.

<Canvas of={Stories.Help} />

### Disabled and read-only

`isDisabled` on the group draws all of it at half; on one checkbox, that one alone. Read-only, the
options can be reached and not turned.

<Canvas of={Stories.States} />

### Validation

`validate` takes the list of values and returns what is wrong, or nothing; `isInvalid` with your
own `FieldError` words shows what the server said. The boxes' edges turn the danger colour; the
words keep theirs.

<Canvas of={Stories.Validation} />

### On a grey surface

The group's `variant` reaches its checkboxes: secondary fills each box with the surface inside its
edge, so an empty one shows on a grey card.

<Canvas of={Stories.OnASurface} />

### In a form

Its `name` sends every value that is on, once each, as a form's repeated field. A required group
with none on stops the form and asks for one, «دست‌کم یکی را انتخاب کنید.», in the page's language
whatever the browser's.

Each `Checkbox` takes its `value` and its own `isDisabled`; the group owns whether it is on.

## Keyboard

Tab goes to each checkbox in turn, and Space turns it.

## Accessibility

The group is a group named by its `Label` and described by its `Description`; each checkbox is
named by its own words. A required group says so on its label, not on each option.

## Styling

`.checkbox-group`; its checkboxes as on the Checkbox page; `[data-invalid]` and `[data-disabled]`.

## As a block

A tree draws the same group, on the web and in Flutter, `orientation` giving a row. Its own check,
one on when required, is written in the page's language.
