# LinkButton

A link drawn as a button: the main way on from a page, such as "Add product", or a cart's way to
checkout. It goes somewhere, as a link does, and can be opened in a new tab; it looks like the
button it stands in for.

<Controls of={Stories.Playground} />

## When to use it

- **The page's main way on,** at the end of its title, such as "Add product".
- **A step to another page,** such as a cart's "Go to checkout".
- **A page elsewhere, given weight,** such as a gateway's own panel.

Not for an action on this page, such as saving or deleting: use a Button. Not for a link in a
sentence: use a Link.

## Import

```tsx
import { buttonVariants, Link } from '@kaladan/diba-react'
```

## Anatomy

A Link with a button's classes: `buttonVariants` takes a Button's `variant`, `size`, `fullWidth`
and `isIconOnly`.

```tsx
<Link href="/products/new" className={buttonVariants({ variant: 'primary' })}>
  <Plus />
  Add product
</Link>
```

It is drawn as a Button is, with no underline: 32, 36 or 40 tall by size from 768 wide and a
step taller on a phone, its icon 8 from its words.

## Examples

### Looks

A Button's seven: `primary` for the page's one main way on, `secondary` and `tertiary` for the
rest, `outline` and `ghost` quieter still, `danger` and `danger-soft` for a way to something
that cannot be undone.

## Accessibility

It is a link, named by its words or its `aria-label`, and an app's router takes it. A screen
reader calls it a link, so its words should say where it goes.

<Keys rows={[['Enter', 'Follows it.']]} />

## Styling

`.link` with a Button's `.button` classes: `.button--primary` and the rest, `--sm`, `--md`,
`--lg`, `--icon-only` and `--full-width`.

## As a block

A tree draws the same, on the web and in Flutter: a Button's `variant`, `size`, `icon` before or
after its words, `isIconOnly` with a `label` that is its name and tooltip, and `fullWidth`. Its
`href` is held to safe URLs, and `isExternal` marks one that leaves the app, which the host opens.
Its `variant` is `secondary` and its `size` `sm` unless the tree says otherwise.
