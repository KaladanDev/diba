# Admin pages

The admin's pages, composed from Diba's basics: a page's head, the day's figures, a list to work
through, a settings section and a record's page. Each is written out to copy.

## A page's head

`Breadcrumbs` back up, the title as the page's heading (`Text` at `2xl`, semibold) with what it is
under it in the muted colour, and its actions at the end: one primary way on, a LinkButton when it
goes to another page, the rest quieter. On a phone the actions wrap under the title.

<Canvas of={Stories.PageHeads} />

## The day's figures

Each figure on its own `Card`: what it counts, muted, with its icon; its value at `2xl`; and how it
moved, a soft `Chip` in the success's or the danger's tone. A plan's limit is a `Meter` with a
`Link` to raise it.

<Canvas of={Stories.Figures} />

## A list to work through

The page's head, a `SearchField` and a `ToggleButtonGroup` to narrow the list, a `Table` whose
statuses are soft `Chip`s and whose sums sit at the end, and the rows shown and the pages in
`Table.Footer`. When nothing matches, the body says so.

<Canvas of={Stories.ListPage} />

## A settings section

A `Card` with its title and what it governs; `Switch`es with a `Description` of what each does, a
`TextField` and a `Select`, a `Separator` between groups, and the way to save at the card's foot,
cancel quieter than save.

<Canvas of={Stories.Settings} />

## A record's page

Its head with its status and its main action, then what was ordered, a secondary `Table` with its
total in `Table.Foot`, beside who ordered it, an `Avatar` and their number, and where it goes.

<Canvas of={Stories.RecordPage} />
