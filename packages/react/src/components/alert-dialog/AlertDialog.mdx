# AlertDialog

A question asked before something that matters: deleting, discarding, publishing. It covers the
page until it is answered, and only its buttons answer it: a press outside or Escape does nothing
unless you let them.

<Canvas of={Stories.Playground} />
<Controls of={Stories.Playground} />

## When to use it

- **Before something that cannot be undone,** such as deleting a product or a store.
- **Before something that loses work,** such as leaving a page with unsaved changes.
- **Before something others will see at once,** such as publishing a theme.

Not for telling the reader something: use an Alert on the page, or a Toast. Not for a form or a
detail: use a Modal or a Drawer.

## Import

```tsx
import { AlertDialog, Button } from '@kaladan/diba-react'
```

## Anatomy

```tsx
<AlertDialog>
  <Button variant="danger-soft">Delete product</Button>
  <AlertDialog.Backdrop>
    <AlertDialog.Container>
      <AlertDialog.Dialog>
        {({ close }) => (
          <>
            <AlertDialog.Header>
              <AlertDialog.Icon status="danger" />
              <AlertDialog.Heading>Delete “Silk scarf”?</AlertDialog.Heading>
            </AlertDialog.Header>
            <AlertDialog.Body>Its pictures and stock are removed for good.</AlertDialog.Body>
            <AlertDialog.Footer>
              <Button variant="tertiary" onPress={close}>
                Cancel
              </Button>
              <Button variant="danger" onPress={close}>
                Delete
              </Button>
            </AlertDialog.Footer>
          </>
        )}
      </AlertDialog.Dialog>
    </AlertDialog.Container>
  </AlertDialog.Backdrop>
</AlertDialog>
```

### The rest

`AlertDialog.Header`, `.Heading`, `.Body` and `.Footer` take their element's props and a
`className`. `AlertDialog.CloseTrigger` is a CloseButton: give it an `aria-label`.

## Accessibility

It is an `alertdialog`, named by its heading and described by its body. Focus moves into it as it
opens, stays in it while open, and returns to its trigger when it closes; the page under it is
hidden from a screen reader.

## Styling

`.alert-dialog__backdrop` and its `--opaque`, `--blur`, `--transparent`;
`.alert-dialog__container`; `.alert-dialog__dialog` with `--xs` to `--cover` and
`[data-placement]`; `.alert-dialog__header`, `__icon` with `--default` to `--danger`, `__heading`,
`__body`, `__footer` and `__close-trigger`. Its paper is the theme's `--overlay`.

## As a block

A tree asks the same question, on the web and in Flutter: its words, its status and its
trigger, and `onConfirm` and `onCancel`. While `onConfirm`'s work runs, its confirm button spins.
