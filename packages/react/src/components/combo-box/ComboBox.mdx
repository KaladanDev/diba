# ComboBox

A field the reader types into to find an option: a city among dozens, a customer, a product. As
they type, the menu under it narrows to the options that hold their words.

<Canvas of={Stories.Playground} />
<Controls of={Stories.Playground} />

## When to use it

- **One of a long list the reader knows by name:** a city, a province, a customer.
- **A value that may be one of a list or the reader's own:** a colour, a district.

For a short list to pick from without typing, use a Select; to search a page and send the words,
a SearchField.

## Import

```tsx
import { ComboBox, Description, FieldError, Input, Label, ListBox } from '@kaladan/diba-react'
```

## Anatomy

```tsx
<ComboBox>
  <Label>City</Label>
  <ComboBox.InputGroup>
    <Input placeholder="Type a city" />
    <ComboBox.Trigger />
  </ComboBox.InputGroup>
  <ComboBox.Popover>
    <ListBox>
      <ListBox.Item id="tehran">Tehran</ListBox.Item>
      <ListBox.Item id="isfahan">Isfahan</ListBox.Item>
    </ListBox>
  </ComboBox.Popover>
  <Description>For the delivery fee.</Description>
  <FieldError />
</ComboBox>
```

## Examples

### Typing narrows it

The menu opens as the reader types and keeps the options whose words hold theirs, anywhere in
them, ignoring case and accents. Choosing one writes its words in the field. Give each option
`textValue`, the words it is found by, when it holds more than text.

<Canvas of={Stories.Filtering} />

### Sections, and nothing found

`ListBox.Section` with a `Header` titles a group of options, which stays while any of its options
match. With `allowsEmptyCollection`, the menu stays open when nothing matches and the list's
`renderEmptyState` says so.

<Canvas of={Stories.Sections} />

### Words of the reader's own

`allowsCustomValue` lets what is typed stand when no option matches: the field keeps it, and
`selectedKey` is `null`. Without it, leaving the field puts back the chosen option's words.
Choosing an option writes its words in. Control the words, or the choice, or neither: with both
controlled, React Aria leaves the words to you, and `onSelectionChange` must write them.

<Canvas of={Stories.CustomValue} />

### Disabled, read-only, invalid, on a grey surface

Read-only, its words can be read and copied; the menu does not open.

<Canvas of={Stories.States} />

### In a form

Its `name` sends the chosen option's `id`, or the words when it allows its own. A required one
left empty stops the form and says so under it, «این فیلد الزامی است.», in the page's language.

## Accessibility

The field is a combo box named by its `Label`; the menu is a list box, and the option the arrow
keys are on is read as they move, the focus staying in the field.

## Styling

`.combo-box` with `--full-width`; `__input-group`, `__trigger` and `__popover`; `[data-open]`,
`[data-invalid]` and `[data-disabled]`. The options are the ListBox's.

## As a block

A tree draws the same field, on the web and in Flutter, its options `ListBoxItem`s and titled
`ListBoxSection`s; `emptyMessage` says when nothing matches. It sends the chosen id, and the words
as they are typed.
