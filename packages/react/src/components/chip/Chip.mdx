# Chip

A short label that says what something is: an order's status, a product's discount. It does
nothing when pressed.

<Canvas of={Stories.Playground} />
<Controls of={Stories.Playground} />

## When to use it

- **A status,** such as an order's, in its tone: waiting, paid, shipped, cancelled.
- **What is special about something,** such as a product's discount or its free delivery.

Not for something to choose or remove, such as a filter: use a TagGroup. Not for a count on an
icon: use a Badge.

## Import

```tsx
import { Chip } from '@kaladan/diba-react'
```

## Anatomy

```tsx
<Chip variant="soft" color="success">
  <Truck />
  <Chip.Label>Shipped</Chip.Label>
</Chip>
```

## Examples

### Looks

`soft`, the default, is the status at 12% over the surface (22% in dark) with its ink, the one to
use for a status. `secondary` is the second surface with the ink; `primary` the solid fill;
`tertiary` clear inside a hairline.

## Accessibility

It is words, read where they stand. Its colour says nothing a screen reader hears, so its words
must say it: "Cancelled", not a red chip alone.

## Styling

`.chip` with `--primary`, `--secondary`, `--soft`, `--tertiary`, its tone (`--success` and the
rest) and `--sm`, `--md`, `--lg`; `.chip__label`.

## As a block

A tree draws the same chip, on the web and in Flutter: its words, an `icon` from the icon list,
`variant`, `color` and `size`. It is `soft` and `sm` unless the tree says otherwise, as by hand.
