# Switch

A setting on or off that takes effect as it is turned: the shop open, test mode, a product shown.
It is Material's switch on both platforms, drawn and moving as Flutter's does.

<Canvas of={Stories.Playground} />
<Controls of={Stories.Playground} />

## When to use it

- **A setting that applies at once,** with no form to send: the shop open or closed, a product
  shown or hidden in a list.

For a choice sent with a form, or agreeing to something, use a Checkbox; for several related
settings, a SwitchGroup.

## Import

```tsx
import { Description, Label, Switch } from '@kaladan/diba-react'
```

## Anatomy

```tsx
<Switch>
  <Switch.Content>
    <Switch.Control>
      <Switch.Thumb />
    </Switch.Control>
    <Label>The shop is open</Label>
  </Switch.Content>
  <Description>Closed, buyers only see the products.</Description>
</Switch>
```

## Examples

### Sizes

`size` scales Material's switch: 0.69 at `sm`, 0.8 at `md` and 0.91 at `lg`, as Flutter's renderer
draws it. `sm` suits a row in a list.

<Canvas of={Stories.Sizes} />

### Off, on, disabled, read-only

Disabled, the whole switch is drawn at half, its thumb keeping its colour. Read-only, it can be
reached and not turned.

<Canvas of={Stories.States} />

### Label and help

Long words wrap beside the track. A switch with no words, a row's in a list, is named with
`aria-label`: say what it turns on, not "on" or "off".

<Canvas of={Stories.LabelAndHelp} />

### In a form

On, it sends its `value` under its `name`, `on` unless it says otherwise; off, nothing.

## Accessibility

A screen reader meets a switch named by its words and read as on or off. The whole switch takes
the pointer, its words and help included.

## Styling

`.switch` with `--sm`, `--md` and `--lg`; `__content`, `__control`, `__thumb` and `__icon`;
`[data-selected]`, `[data-pressed]` and `[data-disabled]`. The track is `--db-control-track` off
and `--db-control-fill` on.

## As a block

A tree draws the same switch, on the web and in Flutter. Alone it sends `true` or `false`; an
`errorMessage` is drawn under it.
