# Empty states

What a list, a card or a page says with nothing in it: that nothing is there, why, and what to do
about it. It is composed from `Stack`, `Text` and a button, with no component of its own.

```tsx
<Stack align="center" gap={4} style={{ padding: 8 }}>
  <Stack align="center" gap={2}>
    <Inbox size={32} color="var(--muted)" aria-hidden />
    <Text size="sm" weight="medium" align="center">
      No orders yet
    </Text>
    <Text size="sm" tone="muted" align="center">
      Share your shop to get the first one.
    </Text>
  </Stack>
  <Button size="sm" variant="secondary">
    Copy the shop’s address
  </Button>
</Stack>
```

Its icon is 32 and muted, its title 14 on 20 and medium in the text's colour, its help 14 on 20
and muted, 8 apart and centred; what to do about it 16 under them; all 8 in. A block tree builds it
the same way, from Stack, Text and Button blocks. Say what is empty in the title, not only with
the picture, which a screen reader does not read.
