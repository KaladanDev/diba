# Separator

A line between sections: across, between a list's lines and its total, or down, between things in
a row. It says the two sides belong apart, and a screen reader hears it as a separator.

<Canvas of={Stories.Playground} />
<Controls of={Stories.Playground} />

## When to use it

- **Between a list and what sums it,** such as a checkout's lines and its total.
- **Between a card's sections,** such as an order's details and its history.
- **Between groups of controls in a row,** such as a toolbar's.

Not for room alone: use a gap, or a Spacer. Not between every row of a list: a list's rows are
spaced, not ruled.

### For the surface under it

`default` is the separator's colour, for a page's card. On a card inside a card, `secondary` is a
step darker, and `tertiary` a step further, so the line still shows on a darker surface: each is
the surface's own colour mixed toward its text.

<Canvas of={Stories.Variants} />

## Where it goes

In a Toolbar a separator runs across the toolbar, whatever it is told: down in a row of buttons,
across in a column.

## Accessibility

It is a `separator`, with its orientation, so a screen reader says where one section ends. It holds
nothing and takes no focus.

## Styling

`.separator` with `--horizontal`, `--vertical`, `--default`, `--secondary` and `--tertiary`. Its
colour is the theme's `--separator`.

## As a block

A tree draws the same line, on the web and in Flutter, across or down; in a Toolbar block it runs
across the toolbar.
