# Accordion

Sections that open and close in place: a page's questions and answers, an order's details, settings
grouped by what they touch. Each item is a heading whose trigger shows or hides its panel, so a
long page reads as a list of titles until the reader opens the one they want.

<Canvas of={Stories.Playground} />
<Controls of={Stories.Playground} />

## When to use it

- **A long page the reader scans:** help pages, product details, settings, filters on a phone.
- **Details on demand:** an order's history or a product's specifications, under what matters most.

Not for the content most readers need: keep that open, or in a Card. To switch between views of
the same thing use Tabs, and for one section on its own an Accordion of one item (a disclosure).

## Import

```tsx
import { Accordion } from '@kaladan/diba-react'
```

## Anatomy

```tsx
<Accordion>
  <Accordion.Item id="shipping">
    <Accordion.Heading>
      <Accordion.Trigger>
        How long does delivery take?
        <Accordion.Indicator />
      </Accordion.Trigger>
    </Accordion.Heading>
    <Accordion.Panel>
      <Accordion.Body>Two to four working days.</Accordion.Body>
    </Accordion.Panel>
  </Accordion.Item>
</Accordion>
```

## Examples

### Variants

`default` lies on the page, a line between its items. `surface` lies on a card's paper, 24 round,
for a section of its own or a page with a grey background.

<Canvas of={Stories.Variants} />

### One open, or several

By default opening one item closes the one open; `allowsMultipleExpanded` lets the reader keep as
many open as they want. `defaultExpandedKeys` names the items open at first.

<Canvas of={Stories.Expansion} />

### Controlled

Hold the open items yourself with `expandedKeys` and `onExpandedChange`, to open them all from a
button, keep them in the address or restore them on return.

<Canvas of={Stories.Controlled} />

### Disabled

`isDisabled` on the accordion stops every item; on an item, only that one. A disabled item's
trigger is faded and skipped by the Tab key.

<Canvas of={Stories.Disabled} />

### Icons and descriptions

<Canvas of={Stories.IconsAndDescriptions} />

### Beside the title

Anything can sit in the trigger: a count, a status chip. Give it `margin-inline-start: auto` and
the indicator none, so the two keep to the end.

### A custom indicator

Pass any element to `Accordion.Indicator`: it takes the indicator's class and `data-expanded`, so
it turns as the item opens, or as your own class says. Put it first to draw it at the start.

## Accessibility

Each trigger is a button with `aria-expanded`, controlling its panel, which is labelled by it; the
trigger sits in a heading, so a screen reader lists the sections among the page's headings. A
closed panel is hidden from it. Motion stops for a reader who asks for less.

## Styling

Every part has a class and the states it is in as data attributes, so a stylesheet can reach any
of them: `.accordion` and `.accordion--surface`, `.accordion__item` (its line is its
`::after`, gone under `[data-hide-separator]`), `.accordion__heading`, `.accordion__trigger`
(`[aria-expanded]`, `[data-focus-visible]`, `[aria-disabled]`), `.accordion__indicator`
(`[data-expanded]`), `.accordion__panel` (`[data-expanded]`, its height in
`--disclosure-panel-height`), `.accordion__body` and `.accordion__body-inner`. Colours are the
theme's: `--foreground`, `--muted`, `--separator`, `--surface`.
