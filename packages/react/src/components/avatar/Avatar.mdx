# Avatar

A person or a business at a glance: their photo, or their initials when there is none, or an icon
for someone unnamed. Without a colour of its own, a name always takes the same one of five hues, so
the same person looks the same on every page.

<Canvas of={Stories.Playground} />
<Controls of={Stories.Playground} />

## When to use it

- **Beside a person's name** in a list, a table row, a comment or a review.
- **On its own** where there is no room for the name, such as who handles an order.
- **For a shop,** with its mark as the photo.

For several people at once, use an AvatarGroup. For a product's picture, use an image, not an
avatar: an avatar is round and stands for someone.

## Import

```tsx
import { Avatar } from '@kaladan/diba-react'
```

## Anatomy

```tsx
<Avatar size="lg" data-hue="emerald" role="img" aria-label="Niloufar Rostami, Online">
  <Avatar.Image src="/staff/niloufar.jpg" alt="" />
  <Avatar.Fallback aria-hidden delayMs={600}>
    NR
  </Avatar.Fallback>
  <span className="avatar__presence" data-presence="online" />
</Avatar>
```

## Examples

### A photo, initials or an icon

The initials stay under the photo, so a photo that fails to load leaves them showing. An unnamed
person, a guest, takes an icon instead.

<Canvas of={Stories.Content} />

### Sizes

`sm`, `md` and `lg`: 32, 40 and 48 across. `md` is the default.

<Canvas of={Stories.Sizes} />

### A colour from the name

Without a `color`, give `data-hue` one of `saffron`, `garnet`, `emerald`, `sky` or `iris`: the
initials take that hue and the fill a tint of it. The block picks it from the name, so a name
always has the same one, on the web and in Flutter.

<Canvas of={Stories.ColourFromTheName} />

### Colours and variants

A `color` is a status tone, for an avatar that says something about the person: `success` for one
who is verified, `danger` for one who is blocked. On the neutral fill its initials take the tone;
`soft`, the fill takes the tone's tint.

<Canvas of={Stories.Colours} />

### Presence

A `.avatar__presence` dot with `data-presence` of `online`, `away`, `busy` or `offline`, at the
bottom of the end side. Say it after the name in the avatar's label, since the dot's colour alone
says nothing to a screen reader.

<Canvas of={Stories.PresenceDot} />

### Ringed

`data-ringed` draws a ring 2 outside the circle, 2 clear of it, in the accent colour: the one
among several that matters here.

<Canvas of={Stories.Ringed} />

## Accessibility

Name the avatar, not its parts: `role="img"` and the person's name in `aria-label`, with the
presence after it ("Niloufar Rostami, Online"); the image's `alt` empty and the initials hidden.
Beside the name in words, hide the avatar instead (`aria-hidden`), so the name is read once.

## Styling

`.avatar` with `.avatar--sm`, `--md`, `--lg` and `--soft`; `.avatar__image`;
`.avatar__fallback` with `--default` to `--danger`; `.avatar__presence` with `data-presence`. Its
size is `--avatar-size`, and its fill and initials `--avatar-fill` and `--avatar-ink`, so a theme
or a page can set them.

## As a block
