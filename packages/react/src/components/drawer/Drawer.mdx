# Drawer

A panel that slides in from an edge of the window: a record's details beside its list, the
filters for a list, a cart. It closes with its buttons, a press outside, Escape, or a drag toward
its edge.

<Canvas of={Stories.Playground} />
<Controls of={Stories.Playground} />

## When to use it

- **A record's details beside its list,** such as an order opened from the order list, the list
  still there when it closes.
- **What shapes a list,** such as its filters.
- **A shop's cart or menu,** from the side of a storefront, or the bottom of a phone.

Not for a short task in the middle of the page: use a Modal. Not for a panel the hand drags to a
height of its own: use a Sheet.

## Import

```tsx
import { Button, Drawer } from '@kaladan/diba-react'
```

## Anatomy

```tsx
<Drawer>
  <Button variant="secondary">Order 1041</Button>
  <Drawer.Backdrop>
    <Drawer.Content placement="bottom">
      <Drawer.Dialog>
        {({ close }) => (
          <>
            <Drawer.Handle />
            <Drawer.CloseTrigger />
            <Drawer.Header>
              <Drawer.Heading>Order 1041</Drawer.Heading>
            </Drawer.Header>
            <Drawer.Body>{/* the order */}</Drawer.Body>
            <Drawer.Footer>
              <Button onPress={close}>Print label</Button>
            </Drawer.Footer>
          </>
        )}
      </Drawer.Dialog>
    </Drawer.Content>
  </Drawer.Backdrop>
</Drawer>
```

## Examples

Each example is its own frame: press its button to open it. The main ones open as they load.

### Edges

From the `bottom`, the default, or the `top`: the window's width, up to 85% of its height, 16 round
at its inner edge. From a side, `left` or `right`: the window's height, square, 320 wide (384 from 640) and at most 85% of the window. `left` and `right` are the page's start and end, so on a
Persian page `left` slides in from the right edge.

### What closes it

Its buttons, its close button, a press outside, Escape, and a drag toward its edge: from its
handle, heading or footer, past 30% of its size or with a quick flick that way. A drag or a flick
into the page leaves it where it is. On a Persian page a start drawer goes out to the right, its
own edge. `isDismissable={false}` on the
backdrop leaves a press outside and a drag out, and `isKeyboardDismissDisabled` leaves Escape out.

## Motion

It slides the window's height or width in over 250ms, easing out, and back out the same way over
250ms, the backdrop fading with it. With reduced motion it appears and goes at once.

### The rest

`Drawer.Header`, `.Heading`, `.Body`, `.Footer` and `.Handle` take their element's props and a
`className`. `Drawer.CloseTrigger` is a CloseButton, named “Close” in the page's language.

## Accessibility

It is a `dialog`, named by its heading. Focus moves into it as it opens, stays in it while open,
and returns to its trigger when it closes; the page under it is hidden from a screen reader and
does not scroll. The handle is only for the hand: the close button and Escape do what it does.

## Styling

`.drawer__backdrop` and its `--opaque`, `--blur`, `--transparent`; `.drawer__content` with
`--bottom`, `--top`, `--left`, `--right`; `.drawer__dialog` with `[data-placement]`;
`.drawer__header`, `__heading`, `__body`, `__footer`, `__handle` and `__close-trigger`. Its paper is
the theme's `--overlay`; its motion is `--drawer-enter-duration`, `--drawer-exit-duration`,
`--drawer-enter-ease` and `--drawer-exit-ease`.

## As a block

A tree opens the same drawer, on the web and in Flutter, from the Button in its `trigger` slot and
only from it. Its edges are `bottom`, `top`, `start` and `end`, the page's; a bottom one has its
handle, and every one its close button.
