# ColorSwatchPicker

One colour from a few, as swatches: a label's colour, a theme's accent, a product's colours. The
chosen one is ringed in its own colour, with a tick on it. It is a list: the arrow keys move
between the colours, and Space or Enter chooses one.

<Controls of={Stories.Playground} />

## When to use it

- **A colour from a set the app chose,** such as a label's or a theme's.
- **A product's colours** on its page, the sold-out ones disabled.

Not for any colour at all: Diba has no free colour picker. For choices that are not colours, use a
RadioGroup or a ToggleButtonGroup.

## Import

```tsx
import { ColorSwatchPicker } from '@kaladan/diba-react'
```

## Anatomy

```tsx
<ColorSwatchPicker aria-label="Label colour" defaultValue="#1f7a5c">
  <ColorSwatchPicker.Item color="#1f7a5c" aria-label="Emerald">
    <ColorSwatchPicker.Swatch />
    <ColorSwatchPicker.Indicator />
  </ColorSwatchPicker.Item>
</ColorSwatchPicker>
```

## Examples

### Round or square

`circle`, the default, or `square`, its corners rounded.

<Canvas of={Stories.Shapes} />

### Sizes

`xs`, `sm`, `md`, `lg` and `xl`: 16, 24, 32, 36 and 40 across. `md` is the default. The ring is 1
wide at `xs`, 2 at `sm` and `md`, and 3 at `lg` and `xl`.

<Canvas of={Stories.Sizes} />

### A row or a column

`grid`, the default, is a row that wraps when it runs out of room; `stack` is a column.

<Canvas of={Stories.Layouts} />

### The chosen one

The ring takes the colour, the swatch shrinks inside it, and the tick sits on the colour: black on
a light one and white on the rest, whichever is clearer, so it is 4.5:1 or more on any colour.

<Canvas of={Stories.Chosen} />

### Its value, held by the page

`value` and `onChange`, or `defaultValue` alone. A value is a colour string such as `"#2f78b5"`;
`onChange` hands back a colour, whose `toString('hex')` says it as `#2F78B5`.

<Canvas of={Stories.Controlled} />

### A colour that cannot be chosen

`isDisabled` on an item fades it and lets the keys pass over it.

## Accessibility

- **The list is named,** with `aria-label` or by the label beside it through `aria-labelledby`.
- **Each colour is named.** Without an `aria-label`, React Aria names a swatch by what the colour
  looks like, in the page's language, «قرمز پررنگ تیره» or "dark vibrant red". Where the colour has
  a name of its own, as a product's do, give it.
- The arrow keys move between the colours, passing over the disabled ones, and Space or Enter
  chooses the one in focus.

## As a block

A tree sends the colours as `#rrggbb` and gets the chosen one back the same way, on the web and in
Flutter. Each swatch is named by what its colour looks like.
