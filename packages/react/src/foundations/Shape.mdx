# Spacing and shape

Room between things on a step of 4, corners by what a thing is, three shadows, and one ring for the
keyboard's focus. Under each example is what the browser drew it at.

## Space

A `Stack`'s `gap` is a step of 4: `1` is 4, `4` is 16, `16` is 64. Keep to the steps it has, 0 to
6, then 8, 10, 12 and 16; a card's insides are 16 apart, a form's fields 16, a page's sections 24
or 32. Where one space must differ from the rest, a `Spacer` gives it.

<Canvas of={Stories.Space} />

## Corners

A component's corners come with it; a page sets none. Buttons, cards and alerts are 24 round,
fields and chips 12, avatars round. An `Image` takes `radius` from the same steps a tree's picture does: 0, 8,
12, 16 or round.

<Canvas of={Stories.Corners} />

## Elevation

Three shadows, each a variable the theme sets: `--surface-shadow` lifts a card off the page,
`--overlay-shadow` lifts a menu, a popover or a dialog over everything, and `--field-shadow` is a
field's 1 edge, drawn as a shadow so a focused field changes nothing's size. In dark, a card has
no shadow; its paper is lighter than the page instead.

<Canvas of={Stories.Elevation} />

## The focus ring

What the keyboard has reached shows a ring 2 wide in `--focus`, 2 clear of a control, on the
page's colour, so it reads on any fill. A field's ring sits on its own edge. The pointer never
draws it. Drawn here as the components draw it, with the same two shadows.
