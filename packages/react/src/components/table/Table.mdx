# Table

Rows of records under named columns: the orders, a product's variants, a size chart. Rows can be
chosen, sorted and opened; totals sit under their own columns.

<Canvas of={Stories.Playground} />
<Controls of={Stories.Playground} />

## When to use it

- **Records to compare,** such as orders by buyer, date and sum.
- **Records to act on,** chosen one or several at a time, such as printing labels for the day's
  orders.
- **Figures in a grid,** such as a product's variants with their stock and prices, or a size
  chart.

Not for one record's details: a card with its lines reads better. Not for a few things to choose
from: use a list. On a phone, a table wider than the screen scrolls across; for a long list a
phone reads, cards one under the other are kinder.

## Import

```tsx
import { Table } from '@kaladan/diba-react'
```


Columns share the width as a browser's table does: each takes what its widest cell asks, and room
left over goes to each in proportion. A table its cells cannot fit scrolls across in its scroll
container. `align="end"` on a column and its cells puts sums and counts at the end, under their
heading.

## Examples

### Two looks

`primary`, the default, is a grey frame 20 round, the headings on the grey and the rows on a white
body 16 round inside it. `secondary` has no frame: its headings are a grey strip 16 round and its
rows lie on what is under them, for a table on a card.

<Canvas of={Stories.Variants} />

### Choosing rows

`selectionMode` on `Table.Content` is `single` or `multiple`. Put a checkbox with
`slot="selection"` in a first column and in each row's first cell: React Aria names each box in the
page's language after the row it chooses, and the head's box chooses every row. A chosen row is the
brand at 8%; one in `disabledKeys` is drawn at half and cannot be chosen.

<Canvas of={Stories.Choosing} />

### Sorting

`allowsSorting` on a column makes its heading the hand under the pointer; give it a
`Table.SortableColumnHeader` and hold the order with `sortDescriptor` and `onSortChange`. The table
does not sort: sort the rows you give it. The sorted column's chevron is at its heading's end; in a
column aligned to the end, the words stay over the figures and the chevron sits just before them.

<Canvas of={Stories.Sorting} />

### Totals

`Table.Foot` holds rows of totals, under their own columns and semibold on the table's grey. In a
table that chooses, make a total row `isDisabled`, so a choice skips it; it keeps its full colour.

<Canvas of={Stories.Totals} />

### With no rows

`renderEmptyState` on `Table.Body` says what is missing. In an element with `className="table__empty"`
it is drawn as the block draws it: in the middle, muted, 24 above and below, on the white body.

<Canvas of={Stories.Empty} />

### Rows that open a record

`onRowAction` on `Table.Content` is called with a row's id when it is pressed or Enter is pressed
on it; or give each row an `href`, and it is a link. In a table that also chooses, a press on the
row still opens it, and its box or Space chooses it.

<Canvas of={Stories.OpeningARecord} />

### What a cell holds

A cell holds text or anything else: a person with a note under their name, a status chip, the
row's buttons at the end, each icon button named and its name in a Tooltip.

<Canvas of={Stories.Cells} />

### Pages

`Table.Footer` is a strip under the table, for a Pagination with the rows shown.

<Canvas of={Stories.Pages} />

### Columns to resize

Inside a `Table.ResizableContainer`, a column with a `Table.ColumnResizer` is widened or narrowed
by the handle at its end, by the pointer or by the arrow keys. Give columns a `defaultWidth` and a
`minWidth`. On the web only: a block has no resizing.

<Canvas of={Stories.Resizing} />

### Loading more

`Table.LoadMore` at the end of the body calls `onLoadMore` when it comes into view, and shows its
`Table.LoadMoreContent`, a spinner, while `isLoading`. Give the scroll container a height.

<Canvas of={Stories.LoadingMore} />

### The rest

`Table.Cell` takes `align`, as its column; `Table.Body` takes `renderEmptyState`;
`Table.SortableColumnHeader` the column's `sortDirection`;
`Table.LoadMore` `onLoadMore` and `isLoading`. Each takes a `className`.

## Accessibility

It is a `grid`, named by its `aria-label`; the row header's cell names each row, and a chosen row
is `aria-selected`. Tab reaches the table once, at its last focused row, and leaves it; the arrows
move between rows and cells.

## Styling

`.table-root` with `--primary` and `--secondary`; `.table__scroll-container`, `__content`,
`__header`, `__column`, `__sortable-column-header`, `__body`, `__row`, `__cell`, `__foot`,
`__footer`, `__empty` and `__column-resizer`. A chosen row's tint is the brand at 8% over
`--surface`.

## As a block

A tree draws the same table, on the web and in Flutter: its columns, aligned at the start, middle
or end, its rows with their cells, which hold text or blocks, and a TableFooter of totals. It
chooses, sorts and opens rows as the server says, and tells it with `onSelectionChange`,
`onSortChange` and `onRowAction`; `emptyMessage` is said when there are no rows, and `onLoadMore`
adds a button to load more under it.
