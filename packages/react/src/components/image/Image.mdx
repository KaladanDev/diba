# Image

A picture that holds its place before it comes: its box is sized from a ratio and lies on the
second paper, 12 round, and the picture fades in over it. One that does not come says what it was
of, in the muted colour, where a browser would draw a torn picture. A zoomable one opens larger on
a press. It draws with its own styles, so a page needs no utility classes for it.

## When to use it

- **A product's picture,** on a shelf, in a cart, on its own page.
- **A small picture in a row,** such as a product's in the admin's tables.
- **A shop's banner** or a collection's cover.

For a person or a shop's mark, use an Avatar: it is round and falls back to initials. For an icon,
use the icon.

## Import

```tsx
import { Image } from '@kaladan/diba-react'
```

## Examples

### Ratios

`ratio` is the box's width to its height, as CSS writes an aspect ratio: `"1 / 1"`, the default,
`"4 / 3"`, `"16 / 9"`. The box is that shape before the picture comes, so the page does not jump
when it does. `"auto"` takes the picture's own shape once it has come, as a tree's picture with no
ratio does.

<Canvas of={Stories.Ratios} />

### Cover or contain

`cover`, the default, fills the box and crops what does not fit. `contain` shows the whole picture,
the second paper round it.

<Canvas of={Stories.Fit} />

### Loaded, failed, or its own fallback

A picture fades in over 400ms. One that fails shows its `alt`; a decorative one, with an empty
`alt`, shows nothing but the paper. `fallback` puts something of your own there instead.

<Canvas of={Stories.States} />

### Corners

`radius` is `none`, `sm`, `md`, `lg` or `full`: 0, 8, 12, 16 or round. `md`, 12, is the default.
Flush along a card's edge, give it `none`, so the card's own corners round it.

<Canvas of={Stories.Corners} />

### Zoomable

`isZoomable` makes the box a button, with the zoom-in pointer: a press, or Enter or Space, opens
the whole picture in a large Modal, no taller than three quarters of the window, closed by its
close button, Escape or a press outside. For a product's photo on its own page; not for a picture
inside a link, which already goes somewhere. A picture that failed has nothing to open.

## Accessibility

- **`alt` is required.** Say what the picture shows. Where the words beside it already say it, as
  a product's name under its picture, give an empty `alt`, so a screen reader does not say it twice.
- **A picture that is a link** takes its name on the link, as the shelf's do, and an empty `alt`.
- **A picture that fails** is still named by its `alt`, so its words, drawn in its place, are said
  once. With no `src` at all, the words are what is said. A decorative one says nothing.
- **A zoomable picture** is a button named «بزرگ‌نمایی» or "Enlarge" and what it shows, «بزرگ‌نمایی
  شال ابریشم»; the dialog it opens is named after the picture.

## As a block

A tree's picture is the same box, on the web and in Flutter: its corners by `radius`, 12 unless it
says otherwise; its shape by `ratio`, or the picture's own without one; and `isZoomable` opens it
larger on a press.
