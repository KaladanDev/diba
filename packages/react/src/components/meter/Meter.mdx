# Meter

How much of something is used: the shop's storage, its plan's products, the stock left. Its label
and value over a track it fills from the start.

<Canvas of={Stories.Playground} />
<Controls of={Stories.Playground} />

## When to use it

- **What is used of a limit,** such as a plan's products or its storage.
- **How much is left,** such as the few of a product still in stock.

Not for a wait, such as an upload: that is a ProgressBar, which is waited on, where a meter
measures.

## Import

```tsx
import { Label, Meter } from '@kaladan/diba-react'
```

## Anatomy

```tsx
<Meter value={80}>
  <Label>Storage</Label>
  <Meter.Output />
  <Meter.Track>
    <Meter.Fill />
  </Meter.Track>
</Meter>
```

## Examples

### Sizes

`sm`, `md` and `lg`: the track 4, 8 and 12 tall, 2, 4 and 6 round.

<Canvas of={Stories.Sizes} />

### Colours

The brand's by default, a status's solid colour, or the secondary text's colour for `default`. In light the brand's and the warning's
take their deeper ink, so the fill stands 3:1 from its track.
As a limit fills, choose its colour by how full it is: green, then amber, then red.

<Canvas of={Stories.Colors} />

### Its value in words

It shows a percent by default. `valueLabel` says it in words of your own, such as "86 of 100";
`formatOptions` formats it as a number or a currency.

## Accessibility

It is a `meter`, named by its Label, its value read in the words it shows.

## Styling

`.meter` with `--sm`, `--md`, `--lg` and its colour; `.meter__output`, `__track` and `__fill`.

## As a block

A tree draws the same meter, on the web and in Flutter: its `value` and limits, `label` and
`showValue`, `valueLabel` or `formatStyle` (`percent`, `decimal` or `currency`, a toman's in the
page's words), `color` and `size`.
