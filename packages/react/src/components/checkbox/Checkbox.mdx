# Checkbox

One thing on or off, with its words beside it: track the stock, wrap it as a gift, accept the
terms. It is Material's checkbox on both platforms, drawn and moving as Flutter's does.

<Canvas of={Stories.Playground} />
<Controls of={Stories.Playground} />

## When to use it

- **A setting that is on or off,** saved with the rest of a form: track stock, charge tax.
- **Agreeing to something** before going on: the shop's terms.
- **Choosing rows** in a list or a table, several at a time.

For several options under one name, use a CheckboxGroup; for one choice among them, a RadioGroup;
for a setting that takes effect the moment it is turned, a Switch.

## Import

```tsx
import { Checkbox, Description, FieldError, Label } from '@kaladan/diba-react'
```

## Anatomy

```tsx
<Checkbox>
  <Checkbox.Content>
    <Checkbox.Control>
      <Checkbox.Indicator />
    </Checkbox.Control>
    <Label>Track stock</Label>
  </Checkbox.Content>
  <Description>Each sale takes one from the stock.</Description>
  <FieldError />
</Checkbox>
```

## Examples

### Off, on and mixed

`isIndeterminate` draws a dash: some of what it stands for is chosen. It is drawn so whatever
`isSelected` says, until the app clears it. Disabled, the whole option is drawn at half.

<Canvas of={Stories.States} />

### Label and help

Long words wrap beside the box. A checkbox with no words, a row's in a table, is named with
`aria-label`.

<Canvas of={Stories.LabelAndHelp} />

### Choosing all

An "all" box above a list is on when every one is, off when none is, and mixed between; pressing
it chooses all, or none when all were chosen.

<Canvas of={Stories.SelectAll} />

### Validation

`isInvalid` with a `FieldError` says what is wrong under it, in the help's place. The words keep
their colour; the box's edge turns the danger colour.

<Canvas of={Stories.Validation} />

### On a grey surface

`variant="secondary"` fills the box with the surface inside its edge, so an empty one shows on a
grey card; chosen, it is gilt as any.

<Canvas of={Stories.OnASurface} />

### In a form

On, it sends its `value` under its `name`; off, nothing. A required one left off stops the form
and says so under it, «این فیلد الزامی است.», in the page's language whatever the browser's.

## Accessibility

A screen reader meets a checkbox named by its words, described by its help, and read as checked,
not checked or mixed. The whole option takes the pointer, words and help included, so a small box
is not the only target.

## Styling

`.checkbox` with `--primary` and `--secondary`; `__content`, `__control` and `__indicator`;
`[data-selected]`, `[data-indeterminate]`, `[data-invalid]` and `[data-disabled]`.

## As a block

A tree draws the same checkbox, on the web and in Flutter. Alone it sends `true` or `false`; in
a CheckboxGroup, the group sends the chosen values.
