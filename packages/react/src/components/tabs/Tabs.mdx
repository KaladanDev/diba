# Tabs

Sections of one thing, one shown at a time: a product's details, pictures and stock; the orders by
status. The tabs name the sections and stay; the panel under them changes.

<Canvas of={Stories.Playground} />
<Controls of={Stories.Playground} />

## When to use it

- **One thing's sections,** each worth a look on its own, such as a product's details and stock.
- **One list seen several ways,** such as the orders by status, each tab with its count.

Not for steps that follow one another: the reader needs to see where they are. Not for pages of
their own: use links. Not for two or three choices that change a value: use a ToggleButtonGroup.

## Import

```tsx
import { Tabs } from '@kaladan/diba-react'
```

## Anatomy

```tsx
<Tabs>
  <Tabs.ListContainer>
    <Tabs.List aria-label="Product sections">
      <Tabs.Tab id="details">
        Details
        <Tabs.Indicator />
      </Tabs.Tab>
      <Tabs.Tab id="stock">
        Stock
        <Tabs.Indicator />
      </Tabs.Tab>
    </Tabs.List>
  </Tabs.ListContainer>
  <Tabs.Panel id="details">…</Tabs.Panel>
  <Tabs.Panel id="stock">…</Tabs.Panel>
</Tabs>
```

## Examples

### Two looks

`primary`, the default, is a 40 tall track on the neutral fill, the tabs sharing it and the chosen
one a pill on the surface. `secondary` is a row over a line, the chosen tab's line in the accent
colour.

### The rest

`Tabs.List` takes an `aria-label`; `Tabs.Panel` an `id` that matches its tab. Each takes a
`className`.

## Accessibility

The row is a `tablist` and each tab a `tab`, controlling its `tabpanel`. Tab reaches the chosen tab
only; the arrows move between tabs and choose them as they go.

## Styling

`.tabs` with `--secondary`, `--align-start`, `--align-end` and `[data-orientation]`;
`.tabs__list-container`, `__list`, `__tab`, `__indicator`, `__panel` and `__separator`.

## As a block

A tree draws the same tabs, on the web and in Flutter: each Tab block carries its label, an icon,
and the blocks of its panel; `onChange` is told the chosen tab's id.
