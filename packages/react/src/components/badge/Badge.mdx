# Badge

A count, a word or a dot on the corner of something: unread messages on an icon, a tick on an
avatar, "New" on a product's picture. It sits a quarter outside its corner, ringed in the page's
colour so it stands clear of what it is on.

## When to use it

- **How many new things wait behind an icon:** messages, notifications, orders.
- **A dot for something unread or something on,** where the number does not matter.
- **One short word on a picture,** such as "New" or "20% off".

For a count or a status among words, in a tab or a table, use a Chip: a badge always sits on a
corner. For whether a person is online, use the Avatar's own presence dot.

## Examples

### A count, words, an icon or a dot

Write a count in the reader's digits, and past a ceiling as the ceiling and a plus: "99+". With
no children it is a dot; at `sm`, 16 across, it reads as one.

```tsx
const digits = new Intl.NumberFormat(locale)
const count = n > 99 ? `${digits.format(99)}+` : digits.format(n)
```

### Sizes

`sm`, `md` and `lg`: at least 16, 28 and 32 across. Match it to what it sits on.

<Canvas of={Stories.Sizes} />

### Colours and variants

`primary` is the colour, solid; `secondary` the colour's text on the neutral fill; `soft` the
colour's tint. `danger` for what wants attention, `accent` for what is new, `success` for what is
done.

<Canvas of={Stories.ColoursAndVariants} />

### Placements

`placement` is one of the page's four corners, `top-right` by default. They are the left and the
right whatever the language, so turn them by the page's direction: on a Persian page the end is
the left.

```tsx
const { direction } = useLocale()
const end = direction === 'rtl' ? 'top-left' : 'top-right'
```

## Accessibility

A badge's number or colour means nothing on its own. Say it in the name of what it sits on, "Messages,
3 new", and hide the badge with `aria-hidden`. Where what it sits on is not named in words, name
the badge itself: `role="img"` and an `aria-label` such as "3 new messages". A dot needs a name as
much as a count does.

## Styling

`.badge` with `.badge--default` to `--danger`, `--primary`, `--secondary`, `--soft`, `--sm`,
`--md`, `--lg` and `--top-right` to `--bottom-left`; `.badge__label`; `.badge-anchor`. Its fill,
text and ring are `--badge-bg`, `--badge-fg` and `--badge-border`.

## As a block

A tree draws the same badge on the block it holds, on the web and in Flutter. It writes a number
in the reader's digits and past `max` as "99+", and turns `start` and `end` by the page's direction
for you. Its `label` names the badge, as an image, in place of its digits. Its `color` is `danger`
unless the tree says otherwise.
