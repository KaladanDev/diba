# Colours

Diba draws with roles, not with paint: a component asks for "the card" or "a note", and the theme
says which colour that is in light and in dark. The roles are the stylesheet's variable names, set from
Diba's palette in `src/styles/themes/diba/variables.css`; an app that loads `tailwind.css` has
them all. Every value on this page is read from the page as the browser resolved it, with the
palette step it is and its contrast, so it cannot drift from what the components draw.

## Use the role

Write `var(--muted)`, or the utility `text-muted`, never `var(--db-slate-500)`. The role is what
changes between light and dark, what a re-theme moves, and what a grey card makes a step stronger.
A palette step is the same colour everywhere, which is right only for a picture.

| What it is                    | Role                                                   | Light                    | Dark                     |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------ | ------------------------ |
| The page                      | `--background`                                         | slate-50                 | slate-950                |
| A card                        | `--surface`                                            | paper-0                  | slate-900                |
| The second and third surfaces | `--surface-secondary`, `--surface-tertiary`            | slate-100, slate-200     | slate-800, slate-700     |
| An overlay                    | `--overlay`                                            | paper-0                  | slate-900                |
| Body text                     | `--foreground`                                         | slate-800                | slate-100                |
| Secondary text                | `--default-foreground`                                 | slate-700                | slate-200                |
| A note                        | `--muted`                                              | slate-500                | slate-400                |
| A note on a grey card         | `--db-muted-secondary`, `--db-muted-tertiary`          | slate-600, slate-600     | slate-400, slate-300     |
| A link                        | `--link`                                               | slate-700                | slate-300                |
| The neutral fill              | `--default`                                            | slate-200                | slate-800                |
| Borders and separators        | `--border`, `--separator`                              | slate-200                | slate-800                |
| The brand                     | `--accent`, `--accent-foreground`                      | the gilt, iris-800 on it | the gilt, iris-950 on it |
| Status                        | `--success`, `--warning`, `--danger` and `-foreground` | emerald, orange, garnet  | the same, a step lighter |

## The papers and their words

The page, a card, the two greys a card can be, and an overlay, each with its body text and a
note. Body text holds 9.5:1 or more everywhere, the least on the third surface in dark. A note is a step stronger on a grey card, so it
keeps 4.5:1 there too: a card sets `--muted` to `--db-muted-secondary` or `--db-muted-tertiary`
for everything inside it.

## Words

On a card: body text, secondary text, a note, a link and a field's placeholder, all at 4.5:1 or
more. A placeholder is slate-500 on a field (slate-400 in dark), and a step darker on a secondary
field's second surface, so it holds there too.

## Brand and status

Each has a fill, the words on it (`-foreground`), and an ink for when it is drawn on a card
rather than filling it: the `--db-*-emphasis` step, which an alert's title, a soft chip's words and
a link in the brand's colour take. A fill colour is chosen to hold its words, not to be read as
words: the gilt is 1.8:1 on paper.

## Controls

A checkbox, a switch and a slider are on in the gilt with slate-700 on it, and off on the
off-track shade, in both themes. A progress bar's, a meter's and a ring's fill is the brand's or a
status's colour, but in light the gilt and the amber take their emphasis, as they were 1.2:1 and
1.6:1 from the track and a fill must be 3:1.

## The palette

The scales the roles are set from: slate for every neutral, paper for white, saffron for the gilt,
iris for the brand's ink, and emerald, orange, garnet and sky for the statuses. The `--db-bg`,
`--db-fg` and `--db-surface` set and the warm `neutral` scale beside them are older; no component
draws with them.
