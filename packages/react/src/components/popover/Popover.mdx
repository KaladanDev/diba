# Popover

A small panel beside its trigger: what a term means, a field to change in place, a link to copy.
It opens from its trigger and closes with a press outside, Escape, or whatever it holds.

<Canvas of={Stories.Playground} />
<Controls of={Stories.Playground} />

## When to use it

- **A term explained where it is used,** such as the platform's fee beside the total.
- **A quick change in place,** such as a product's stock in its row of the list.
- **A few things to do with one item,** such as sharing a product's link.

Not for a list of actions: use a Dropdown. Not for a few words that need no pressing: use a
Tooltip. Not for a form of more than a field or two: use a Modal.

## Import

```tsx
import { Button, Popover } from '@kaladan/diba-react'
```

## Anatomy

```tsx
<Popover>
  <Button variant="ghost">Fee</Button>
  <Popover.Content placement="bottom">
    <Popover.Dialog>
      <Popover.Arrow />
      <Popover.Heading>What is the fee?</Popover.Heading>
      <p>2% of each order, after settlement.</p>
    </Popover.Dialog>
  </Popover.Content>
</Popover>
```

### Placements

A side of its trigger: `top`, `bottom`, `start` or `end`. Start and end follow the page's
direction, so `start` is the right of a Persian page. It turns to the other side only when it does
not fit on its own and the other has more room, and keeps 8 clear of the window's edges.

<Canvas of={Stories.Placements} />

### Where along the side

After the side, where along it: `bottom start` lines its start up with the trigger's start,
`bottom end` its end with the trigger's end, and `bottom` centres it.

<Canvas of={Stories.Alignment} />

### An arrow

It sits 8 from its trigger; with `Popover.Arrow` the arrow fills that gap, pointing at the trigger's
middle.

<Canvas of={Stories.Arrow} />

### Leaving the page usable

By default it keeps the focus and covers the page until it closes, as a task does. `isNonModal`
leaves the page usable while it is open, as a hint does; a press outside still closes it.

<Canvas of={Stories.NonModal} />

### Controlled

Hold whether it is open with `isOpen` and `onOpenChange`.

<Canvas of={Stories.Controlled} />

### From any element

A Button is its trigger as it is. Anything else goes in `Popover.Trigger`, which makes it pressable
and lets the keyboard reach it.

### The rest

`Popover.Heading` and `Popover.Arrow` take their element's props and a `className`; give the arrow
an element of your own as its child to draw another. `Popover.Trigger` takes a `div`'s props.

## Accessibility

It is a `dialog`, named by its heading. Focus moves into it as it opens and returns to its trigger
when it closes; while it is open, unless it is non-modal, the page outside is hidden from a screen
reader.

## Styling

`.popover` with `[data-placement]`, `.popover__dialog`, `.popover__heading`, `.popover__trigger`;
the arrow is `[data-slot="popover-overlay-arrow"]`, filled with the theme's `--overlay`. Its shadow
is `--overlay-shadow`.

## As a block

A tree opens the same popover, on the web and in Flutter, from the Button in its `trigger` slot and
only from it. Its placements are the schema's: a side and then `start` or `end` along it.
