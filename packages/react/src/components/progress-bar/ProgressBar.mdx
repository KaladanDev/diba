# ProgressBar

How far along something is: photos uploading, a spreadsheet's rows coming in, the way to free
delivery. Its label and value over a track that fills from the start.

<Canvas of={Stories.Playground} />
<Controls of={Stories.Playground} />

## When to use it

- **A wait you can measure,** such as an import's rows or an upload.
- **The way to a goal,** such as a cart's way to free delivery or a month's sales target.

Not for a wait with no measure: use a Spinner, or `isIndeterminate`. Not for how much of
something is used, such as storage: that is a Meter, which measures rather than waits.

## Import

```tsx
import { Label, ProgressBar } from '@kaladan/diba-react'
```

## Anatomy

```tsx
<ProgressBar value={60}>
  <Label>Uploading photos</Label>
  <ProgressBar.Output />
  <ProgressBar.Track>
    <ProgressBar.Fill />
  </ProgressBar.Track>
</ProgressBar>
```

## Examples

### Sizes

`sm`, `md` and `lg`: the track 4, 8 and 20 tall.

<Canvas of={Stories.Sizes} />

### Colours

The brand's by default, a status's solid colour, or the neutral fill for `default`. In light the brand's and the warning's
take their deeper ink, so the fill stands 3:1 from its track.

<Canvas of={Stories.Colors} />

### No telling how far

`isIndeterminate` runs two lines along it, each end on its own curve, as Material's does; on a
Persian page they run from the right.

<Canvas of={Stories.Indeterminate} />

### Its value in words

It shows a percent by default. `valueLabel` says it in words of your own, such as "84 of 120";
`formatOptions` formats it as a number or a currency.

## Accessibility

It is a `progressbar`, named by its Label, its value read with `aria-valuetext` in the words it
shows. An indeterminate one has no value.

## Styling

`.progress-bar` with `--sm`, `--md`, `--lg` and its colour; `.progress-bar__output`, `__track` and
`__fill`.

## As a block

A tree draws the same bar, on the web and in Flutter: its `value` and limits, `label` and
`showValue`, `valueLabel` or `formatStyle` (`percent`, `decimal` or `currency`, a toman's in the
page's words), `color`, `size` and `isIndeterminate`.
